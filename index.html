WHY THIS MATTERS

When a farmer can no longer carry the burden alone.

Older cows, rising feed costs, health expenses, retirement and changing family circumstances can make animal care difficult. The answer should protect both sides of the relationship: the farmer's dignity and the cow's welfare.

THE COW HOSTEL MODEL

A simple pathway from need to care.

The hostel creates a structured place for safe, responsible cattle care — with a clear review process and room to grow sustainably.

01
Request

Farmer shares basic details about the cow and situation.

02
Assessment

Foundation reviews eligibility, condition and available capacity.

03
Safe transfer

Approved cattle are transferred using an agreed care process.

04
Ongoing care

Shelter, feed, water, health checks and daily attention continue.

🏠Safe shelterClean · ventilated · weather-protected
💧Fresh waterReliable access throughout the day
🥬Nutritious feedPlanned feeding and special needs
🩺Veterinary careMonitoring and treatment when needed
