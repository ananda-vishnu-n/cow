Welcome

A Place Where Compassion Comes First

At Goshala, every cow is given a safe place to rest, nutritious food, clean water and attentive daily care. From maintaining a clean and comfortable shelter to providing healthcare when needed, we are committed to creating an environment where every cow can live with dignity and peace.

We believe that caring for animals is more than providing shelter — it is about understanding their needs, respecting their lives and being there for them every day.
